# SNAP Mini Power Controller

KiCad 10 project for a minimal solar charger and power distribution board, sized to power a mini handheld LoRa mesh node. It has solder pads for up to four 5 V solar panels (one shared return), a 1S Li-ion battery, a load, and an off-board switch in series with battery + toward the load. Each panel + reaches VIN through its own 1N5819WS Schottky. A CN3163 linear charger charges the cell, with red (charging) and green (done) status LEDs behind a normally open solder jumper. An AO3401A P-MOSFET in the battery + line guards against a reversed cell. 20 × 15 mm, two M2 mounting holes in the top corners, 2 layers. No net carries more than 1 A.

- KiCad project: `SNAP_Power_Mini.kicad_pro` at the repo root (folder name matches the project name, which the kicad MCP server relies on). The `.kicad_pro` was copied from SolarNodeChargeController so the design rules match (0.2 mm clearance, 0.5 mm copper-to-edge, 0.3 mm min drill, 0.8 mm min silk text).
- Schematic: `SNAP_Power_Mini.kicad_sch`, single A4 sheet, generated by `tools/gen-schematic.js`.
- PCB: `SNAP_Power_Mini.kicad_pcb`, generated by `tools/build-pcb.py` (placement, tracks, vias, zones, silk, outline in one script).
- Symbol library: `SNAP_Power_Mini.kicad_sym` (project `sym-lib-table`), the CN3163, written by the schematic generator.
- Footprint library: `SNAP_Power_Mini.pretty` (project `fp-lib-table`), written by `tools/make-footprints.py`: `WirePad_1x02_SMD_1.4x1.5mm_P2.0mm` (J1–J3), `WirePad_Solar4_SMD_1.4x1.5mm_GND2.8x1.5mm` (J4: four 1.4 × 1.5 mm panel + pads spread over 14.5 mm and a 2.8 × 1.5 mm return in the middle; pad order 1, 2, 5, 3, 4 so the schematic keeps pin 5 = GND at the bottom), `SolderJumper-2_P0.9mm_Open_Pad0.6x0.8mm` (JP1) and `MountingHole_2.1mm_M2` (2.1 mm NPTH, 4 mm courtyard for the screw head).
- Images: `docs/images/` — `schematic.svg`, `pcb-top.png`, `pcb-bottom.png`, `pcb-iso.png`. `output/` (gitignored) holds ERC/DRC reports, the netlist and the fab package.

Design decisions:
- **Modelled on the SD05CRMA module** (CN3163, 4.4–6.5 V solar input, 1 A). No schematic of the module was found online. The circuit follows the datasheet's Figure 1, which such modules implement, including its two status LEDs: each open-drain output sinks an LED + resistor from VIN. The LEDs therefore light only while the panel supplies power; both are dark at night or with no panel.
- **0402 for the LEDs and their resistors:** 0603 parts did not fit beside the IC without growing the board; at 0402 the two LED rows fit between the BAT+ via at pin 8 and C2.
- **Panel OR-ing:** each panel + has its own Schottky into VIN, so a shaded or unplugged panel can't sink current from the lit ones, and a panel wired backwards is blocked. The cost is ~0.3–0.4 V of drop, which a 5 V panel can spare above the CN3163's 4.4 V minimum. 1 A diodes because a single panel may carry the whole charge current.
- **Reverse-battery P-FET (Q1):** drain to the cell, source to BAT+, gate to GND. A correctly connected cell forward-biases the body diode, which pulls the source up to Vbat − 0.7 V, so Vgs ≈ −Vbat turns the channel on; from then on it conducts both ways (~60 mΩ at 4.5 V, ~85 mΩ at 2.5 V), so the charger still charges through it. A reversed cell sees an off FET with its body diode reverse-biased. No gate resistor or zener: |Vgs| ≤ 4.2 V against a 12 V rating. It sits on the cell side of everything, so the charger, switch and load are all protected.
- **LED enable jumper (JP1):** normally open, in the shared VIN feed to R2/R3, so the LEDs cost nothing unless bridged. One jumper for both LEDs (CHRG and DONE are never low together).
- **TEMP grounded** disables battery temperature sensing (a bare cell has no NTC). **FB tied to BAT** gives the fixed 4.2 V float, with no Rx.
- **Charger on the cell side of the switch:** the CN3163 BAT pin feeds BAT+ directly, so the cell charges with the switch open and the switch only cuts the load. The alternative (charger after the switch) would power the load from the panel with the switch off.
- **Input limits:** CN3163 absolute max is 6.5 V on every pin; the operating input is 4.4–6 V. Use 5 V panels whose open-circuit voltage stays below 6.5 V; the diode drop is small at no load, so don't count on it. "6 V" panels (Voc ≈ 7 V+) will damage it. No reverse-polarity or TVS protection, same as the SD05CRMA; the datasheet suggests a TVS or Schottky for long panel cables.
- **Heat:** a linear charger dissipates (VIN − VBAT) × I, up to ~1.5–2 W at 1 A from a 5–6 V panel into a low cell. On a board this small the CN3163 will hit its ~130 °C thermal regulation and reduce the current by itself. That is expected behaviour, not a fault. Lower R1's current (raise R1) if that's a problem.
- **Symbol pin types:** CHRG/DONE are open_collector. VIN has a PWR_FLAG (it's fed by the diodes' passive pins); BAT+ has none (U1 BAT is power_out).
- J-pads are `Connector_Generic:Conn_01x02` / `Conn_01x05` with `in_bom no` (JP1's symbol is `in_bom no` in the library); their footprint is `exclude_from_bom`/`exclude_from_pos_files`, and the two must agree or DRC parity complains.

## Board

20 × 15 mm, sharp corners (V-score panel friendly), board top-left at (100, 100) in the editor. Copper is ≥0.5 mm from every edge; both GND zone outlines are inset 0.5 mm (an outline on the edge is itself a DRC error).

- **Holes:** H1/H2 at (2, 2) and (18, 2), 2.1 mm.
- **Top row (y = 1.3):** J1 −/+ at x = 5 / 7, J2 at 9 / 11, J3 +/− at 13 / 15. Pads 1.4 × 1.5 mm, 2 mm pitch.
- **Q1 (8, 4.75) rot 180**, under the BAT/SW pads and above the IC: drain (pin 3) at (7.06, 4.75) under J1.1, source (pin 2) at (8.94, 3.8) under J2.1, gate (pin 1) at (8.94, 5.7). Its silk outline just clears the `-BAT+` label and its courtyard the IC's.
- **Diode columns** under the holes, vertical SOD-323: D4 (S2) at (1.5, 5.7) and D3 (S1) at (1.5, 9.05) on the left; D5 (S3) at (18.7, 5.7) and D6 (S4) at (18.7, 9.05) on the right. Anodes face the ends of each column, cathodes (VIN) the middle, where they're joined. The column tops just clear the hole courtyards (r = 2 mm).
- **Middle band:** U1 at (10, 9.25) rot 0 (VIN pin 4 bottom-left, BAT pin 5 bottom-right). R1 at (4.6, 8.615) in line with ISET. C1 at (4.7, 11.0) next to pin 4. C2 at (15.3, 11.0) next to pin 5. Right of U1, two rows of 0402 status parts: D1 (14.75, 8.4) + R2 (16.7, 8.4) on CHRG, D2 (14.75, 9.6) + R3 (16.7, 9.6) on DONE, cathodes toward the IC. JP1 at (16.0, 7.2), between the VIN bus and the LED rows: VIN pad (left) up to the bus, LED_PWR pad (right) to R2/R3.
- **Solar row along the bottom edge (y = H − 1.3 = 13.7):** J4 S1+, S2+, GND, S3+, S4+ at x = 1.25, 5.275, 10, 14.725, 18.75, 0.55 mm from the side edges; the GND pad is 2.8 mm wide. Labels sit above the pads at y = H − 2.58, between the pads and the U1/C1/C2 pads; `GND` sets the board height, since it has to clear U1's bottom silk line.
- **Hole keepouts:** rule areas r = 2.0 mm around each hole on both layers (no pour, tracks or vias) so a 4 mm screw head or standoff never touches copper.

Routing (all hand-placed in `TRACKS` / `VIAS`; 0.4 mm power, 0.3 mm panel and GND stubs, 0.25 mm signals, 0.6/0.3 mm vias):
- **F.Cu:** a 1.0 mm bridge J2.2→J3.1 (VOUT). J1.1 straight down to Q1's drain; Q1's source up to J2.1 and along y = 3.8 to the BAT+ via at (13.85, 5.0). S1 and S4 run straight down from the lower diodes' anodes to the outer solar pads. The VIN bus (0.4 mm) runs straight along y = 6.4, between Q1 and the IC, from the left cathode pair to the right one, with a stub down to JP1's VIN pad. FB pin 8 and BAT pin 5 each go to a BAT+ via just right of the pin; pin 5's track continues to C2. CHRG/DONE run to the LED cathodes; JP1's LED pad feeds R2.2/R3.2 along y = 7.2 and down x = 17.21.
- **B.Cu jumpers:** S2 (via by D4's anode at (2.7, 5.1), down x = 2.7 to a via in the S2 pad), S3 (via by D5's anode at (17.6, 5.1), down x = 17.6 to the S3 pad), BAT+ (via at (13.85, 5.0) down x = 13.85 joining the pin 8 and pin 5 vias), VIN (bus via at (5.6, 7.1) down to the via between C1 and pin 4).
- **Pours:** GND on both layers, solid pad connection. Six thermal vias in U1's exposed pad tie it to the B.Cu plane (datasheet layout note 4). The solar GND pad has two vias. BAT− sits in an F.Cu pour island left of Q1 with its own via at (4.8, 4.8); OUT− and Q1's gate reach the B.Cu plane through the vias at (10.3, 5.7) and (11.1, 5.7).

Silkscreen: front — `-BAT+` / `SW` / `+OUT-` under the top row and `S1+ S2+ GND S3+ S4+` over the solar row at the bottom edge (0.8 mm, ≥0.1 mm from the pads). JP1 has no label (no room; the README points to it). Component references are hidden (no room). Back — "JaisorGadgets v2.0" (0.9 mm, between the holes) / "SNAP Mini Power Controller" (0.8 mm, 16.7 mm wide, below them, clear of the vias at y = 4.8–5.7); the script shrinks a line only if it outgrows its room (between the holes, or nearly the full width below them), so re-check it against the vias after moving any.

## Regenerating the design

From the repo root:

```bash
"C:/Program Files/KiCad/10.0/bin/python.exe" tools/make-footprints.py   # only after changing a footprint
node tools/gen-schematic.js
kicad-cli sch erc --format json --severity-all -o output/erc.json SNAP_Power_Mini.kicad_sch
kicad-cli sch export netlist --format kicadxml -o output/net.xml SNAP_Power_Mini.kicad_sch
"C:/Program Files/KiCad/10.0/bin/python.exe" tools/build-pcb.py output/net.xml
kicad-cli pcb drc --schematic-parity --severity-all --format json -o output/drc.json SNAP_Power_Mini.kicad_pcb
```

`build-pcb.py` prints the pad centres of U1, R1–R3, C1/C2, D1–D6, JP1 and J4; the track coordinates in `TRACKS` assume those positions, so re-check them if a placement changes.

Images:

```bash
kicad-cli sch export svg --exclude-drawing-sheet -o docs/images SNAP_Power_Mini.kicad_sch   # then rename SNAP_Power_Mini.svg -> schematic.svg
kicad-cli pcb render --side top --quality high -w 1600 -h 1000 -o docs/images/pcb-top.png SNAP_Power_Mini.kicad_pcb
kicad-cli pcb render --side bottom --quality high -w 1600 -h 1000 -o docs/images/pcb-bottom.png SNAP_Power_Mini.kicad_pcb
kicad-cli pcb render --side top --quality high --rotate "-40,0,25" --perspective -w 1600 -h 1000 -o docs/images/pcb-iso.png SNAP_Power_Mini.kicad_pcb
```

KiCad has no 3D model for the CN3163's footprint variant, so the renders show only its pads.

- Each script overwrites its output. **Once anyone edits the schematic or board in KiCad, stop regenerating that file**: port the edits into the scripts first, or switch to editing in KiCad.
- Before writing, check the editor for that file is closed (`~*.kicad_sch.lck` / `~*.kicad_pcb.lck`); afterwards the user must close the editor without saving and reopen.
- UUIDs in the schematic are deterministic (SHA-1 of the item key), so regenerating keeps the PCB footprint links stable.
