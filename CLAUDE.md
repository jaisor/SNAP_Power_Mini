# SolarMeshMiniPDB

KiCad 10 project for a minimal solar charger and power distribution board, sized to power a mini handheld LoRa mesh node. It has solder pads for a 5 V solar panel, a 1S Li-ion battery, a load, and an off-board switch in series with battery + toward the load. A CN3163 linear charger charges the cell from the panel, with red (charging) and green (done) status LEDs. 20 × 12 mm, two M2 mounting holes, 2 layers. No net carries more than 1 A.

- KiCad project: `SolarMeshMiniPDB.kicad_pro` at the repo root (folder name matches the project name, which the kicad MCP server relies on). The `.kicad_pro` was copied from SolarNodeChargeController so the design rules match (0.2 mm clearance, 0.5 mm copper-to-edge, 0.3 mm min drill, 0.8 mm min silk text).
- Schematic: `SolarMeshMiniPDB.kicad_sch`, single A4 sheet, generated by `tools/gen-schematic.js`.
- PCB: `SolarMeshMiniPDB.kicad_pcb`, generated by `tools/build-pcb.py` (placement, tracks, vias, zones, silk, outline in one script).
- Symbol library: `SolarMeshMiniPDB.kicad_sym` (project `sym-lib-table`), the CN3163, written by the schematic generator.
- Footprint library: `SolarMeshMiniPDB.pretty` (project `fp-lib-table`), one footprint `WirePad_1x02_SMD_2.0x2.1mm_P3.5mm`.
- Images: `docs/images/` — `schematic.svg`, `pcb-top.png`, `pcb-bottom.png`, `pcb-iso.png`. `output/` (gitignored) holds ERC/DRC reports, the netlist and the fab package.

## Circuit

```
J4 SOLAR 1 ── VIN ─┬─ C1 10u ─ GND        U1 CN3163: VIN(4)  BAT(5)+FB(8) ── BAT+ ─┬─ C2 10u ─ GND
                   └──────────────────────── VIN(4)                                 ├─ J1 BATTERY 1
                                             ISET(2) ─ R1 1.2k ─ GND                └─ J2 SWITCH 1
                                             TEMP(1), GND(3), EP(9) ─ GND
                                             CHRG(7) ─ D1 red   ─ R2 1k ─ VIN
                                             DONE(6) ─ D2 green ─ R3 1k ─ VIN
J2 SWITCH 2 ── VOUT ── J3 LOAD 1          all pad 2s (J1, J3, J4) ── GND
```

Datasheet: Consonance CN3163 REV 1.0 (eSOP-8). Copy used: https://kaspars.net/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/CN3163.pdf. Pinout 1 TEMP, 2 ISET, 3 GND, 4 VIN, 5 BAT, 6 ~DONE, 7 ~CHRG, 8 FB, exposed pad = GND. The PDF text extraction drops the overbarred names in the pin table; the 6/7 order comes from the description order and was cross-checked against wiki.adom.inc/ray/cn3163.

Design decisions:
- **Modelled on the SD05CRMA module** (CN3163, 4.4–6.5 V solar input, 1 A). No schematic of the module was found online. The circuit follows the datasheet's Figure 1, which such modules implement, including its two status LEDs: each open-drain output sinks an LED + resistor from VIN. The LEDs therefore light only while the panel supplies power; both are dark at night or with no panel.
- **0402 for the LEDs and their resistors:** 0603 parts did not fit beside the IC without growing the board; at 0402 the two LED rows fit between the BAT+ via at pin 8 and C2.
- **TEMP grounded** disables battery temperature sensing (a bare cell has no NTC). **FB tied to BAT** gives the fixed 4.2 V float, with no Rx.
- **Charger on the cell side of the switch:** the CN3163 BAT pin feeds BAT+ directly, so the cell charges with the switch open and the switch only cuts the load. The alternative (charger after the switch) would power the load from the panel with the switch off.
- **Input limits:** CN3163 absolute max is 6.5 V on every pin; the operating input is 4.4–6 V. Use a 5 V panel whose open-circuit voltage stays below 6.5 V. "6 V" panels (Voc ≈ 7 V+) will damage it. No reverse-polarity or TVS protection, same as the SD05CRMA; the datasheet suggests a TVS or Schottky for long panel cables.
- **Heat:** a linear charger dissipates (VIN − VBAT) × I, up to ~1.5–2 W at 1 A from a 5–6 V panel into a low cell. On a 20 × 12 mm board the CN3163 will hit its ~130 °C thermal regulation and reduce the current by itself. That is expected behaviour, not a fault. Lower R1's current (raise R1) if that's a problem.
- **Symbol pin types:** CHRG/DONE are open_collector. VIN has a PWR_FLAG (it's fed from a passive pad); BAT+ has none (U1 BAT is power_out).
- J-pads are `Connector_Generic:Conn_01x02` with `in_bom no`; their footprint is `exclude_from_bom`/`exclude_from_pos_files`, and the two must agree or DRC parity complains.

## Board

20 × 12 mm, sharp corners (V-score panel friendly), board top-left at (100, 100) in the editor. Copper is ≥0.5 mm from every edge; both GND zone outlines are inset 0.5 mm (an outline on the edge is itself a DRC error).

- **Top band (pad row centre y = 3.35):** H1/H2 M2 holes at x = 2.3 / 17.7. Four pad pairs at x = 6.1 (J4 SOL), 8.7 (J1 BAT), 11.3 (J2 SW), 13.9 (J3 OUT). Pads 2.0 × 2.1 mm, + row on top, 0.6 mm column gap, 1.4 mm row gap for the labels.
- **Bottom band:** U1 at (10, 9.25) rot 0 (VIN pin 4 bottom-left, BAT pin 5 bottom-right). R1 at (4.6, 8.615) in line with ISET. C1 at (4.7, 11.0) next to pin 4. C2 at (15.3, 11.0) next to pin 5. Right of U1, two rows of 0402 status parts: D1 (14.75, 8.4) + R2 (16.7, 8.4) on CHRG, D2 (14.75, 9.6) + R3 (16.7, 9.6) on DONE, cathodes toward the IC.
- **Hole keepouts:** rule areas r = 2.2 mm around each hole on both layers (no pour, tracks or vias) so a screw head or standoff never touches copper.

Routing (all hand-placed in `TRACKS` / `VIAS`; 0.4 mm power, 0.3 mm GND stubs, 0.25 mm ISET, 0.6/0.3 mm vias):
- **F.Cu:** a 1.2 mm bridge J1.1→J2.1 (BAT+) and one J4.2→J1.2 (GND). C1→via→pin 4 (VIN). FB pin 8 and BAT pin 5 each go to a BAT+ via just right of the pin; pin 5's track continues to C2. CHRG/DONE run 0.25 mm between those vias to the LED cathodes. VIN for the LEDs (0.25 mm) leaves a via on the VIN jumper at (5.6, 6.9), runs along y = 6.7 between the pad rows and the IC, and drops through R2.2 and R3.2. TEMP/GND stubs run into the exposed pad, R1 and C1 returns go to pin 3, and C2's return goes to a via.
- **B.Cu jumpers:** VIN (J4.1 down the left to the via by pin 4), BAT+ (J1.1 down, along y = 6.9, then down x = 13.85 joining the pin 8 and pin 5 vias), VOUT (J2.2 diagonal to J3.1). That is how the switch output crosses the GND row, and why F.Cu right of the IC is free for the LED tracks.
- **Pours:** GND on both layers, solid pad connection. Six thermal vias in U1's exposed pad tie it to the B.Cu plane (datasheet layout note 4). J1.2 and J3.2 each have a via to B.Cu.

Silkscreen: front — `SOL` / `BAT` / `SW` / `OUT` (0.8 mm) in the gap between the pad rows, `+` / `-` beside each row at both ends next to the holes. Component references are hidden (no room). Back — "SolarMeshMiniPDB" / "v2.0 by JaisorGadgets" in the via-free bands; the script shrinks a line only if it outgrows its room (between the holes for the name, nearly the full width for the line below them), so re-check it against the vias after moving any.

## Regenerating the design

From the repo root:

```bash
node tools/gen-schematic.js
kicad-cli sch erc --format json --severity-all -o output/erc.json SolarMeshMiniPDB.kicad_sch
kicad-cli sch export netlist --format kicadxml -o output/net.xml SolarMeshMiniPDB.kicad_sch
"C:/Program Files/KiCad/10.0/bin/python.exe" tools/build-pcb.py output/net.xml
kicad-cli pcb drc --schematic-parity --severity-all --format json -o output/drc.json SolarMeshMiniPDB.kicad_pcb
```

`build-pcb.py` prints the U1, R1–R3, C1/C2 and D1/D2 pad centres; the track coordinates in `TRACKS` assume those positions, so re-check them if a placement changes.

Images:

```bash
kicad-cli sch export svg --exclude-drawing-sheet -o docs/images SolarMeshMiniPDB.kicad_sch   # then rename SolarMeshMiniPDB.svg -> schematic.svg
kicad-cli pcb render --side top --quality high -w 1600 -h 1000 -o docs/images/pcb-top.png SolarMeshMiniPDB.kicad_pcb
kicad-cli pcb render --side bottom --quality high -w 1600 -h 1000 -o docs/images/pcb-bottom.png SolarMeshMiniPDB.kicad_pcb
kicad-cli pcb render --side top --quality high --rotate "-40,0,25" --perspective -w 1600 -h 1000 -o docs/images/pcb-iso.png SolarMeshMiniPDB.kicad_pcb
```

KiCad has no 3D model for the CN3163's footprint variant, so the renders show only its pads.

- Each script overwrites its output. **Once anyone edits the schematic or board in KiCad, stop regenerating that file**: port the edits into the scripts first, or switch to editing in KiCad.
- Before writing, check the editor for that file is closed (`~*.kicad_sch.lck` / `~*.kicad_pcb.lck`); afterwards the user must close the editor without saving and reopen.
- UUIDs in the schematic are deterministic (SHA-1 of the item key), so regenerating keeps the PCB footprint links stable.

## Fabrication

`output/fab/SolarMeshMiniPDB_v2.0_gerbers.zip` (gitignored; attach to a release tag) contains:
- 8 Gerber X2 layers: F/B copper, F.Paste, F/B mask, F/B silkscreen, Edge.Cuts.
- The Gerber job file.
- Separate PTH/NPTH Excellon drill files and drill maps.

Checked:
- The job file says 2 layers / 1.6 mm / 20 × 12 mm (20.05 × 12.05 including the outline stroke).
- PTH = 17 × 0.3 mm vias; NPTH = 2 × 2.2 mm.

Order settings: 2 layers, FR-4 1.6 mm, 1 oz copper, min track/space 0.25/0.2 mm, min drill 0.3 mm. Assembly is top side only: U1, R1–R3, C1, C2, D1, D2. A BOM/CPL for JLCPCB-style assembly is not generated yet.

Regenerate (after DRC is clean):

```bash
OUT=output/fab
kicad-cli pcb export gerbers --layers F.Cu,B.Cu,F.Paste,F.Silkscreen,B.Silkscreen,F.Mask,B.Mask,Edge.Cuts --subtract-soldermask --check-zones -o $OUT/gerbers SolarMeshMiniPDB.kicad_pcb
kicad-cli pcb export drill --format excellon --excellon-units mm --excellon-zeros-format decimal --drill-origin absolute --excellon-separate-th --generate-map --map-format gerberx2 -o $OUT/gerbers/ SolarMeshMiniPDB.kicad_pcb
python -c "import zipfile,os; z=zipfile.ZipFile('$OUT/SolarMeshMiniPDB_v2.0_gerbers.zip','w',zipfile.ZIP_DEFLATED); [z.write(os.path.join('$OUT/gerbers',n),n) for n in sorted(os.listdir('$OUT/gerbers'))]"
```
