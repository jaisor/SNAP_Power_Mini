# SNAP Mini Power Controller

KiCad 10 project for a minimal solar charger and power distribution board, sized to power a mini handheld LoRa mesh node. It has solder pads for a 5 V solar panel, a 1S Li-ion battery, a load, an off-board switch in series with battery + toward the load, and an optional battery NTC. A CN3163 linear charger charges the cell, with red (charging) and green (done) status LEDs behind a normally open solder jumper. An AO3401A P-MOSFET in the battery + line guards against a reversed cell. 14.4 × 12.2 mm, no mounting holes, 2 layers. No net carries more than 1 A.

- KiCad project: `SNAP_Power_Mini.kicad_pro` at the repo root (folder name matches the project name, which the kicad MCP server relies on). The `.kicad_pro` was copied from SolarNodeChargeController so the design rules match (0.2 mm clearance, 0.5 mm copper-to-edge, 0.3 mm min drill, 0.8 mm min silk text).
- Schematic: `SNAP_Power_Mini.kicad_sch`, single A4 sheet, generated by `tools/gen-schematic.js`.
- PCB: `SNAP_Power_Mini.kicad_pcb`, generated by `tools/build-pcb.py` (placement, tracks, vias, zones, silk, outline in one script).
- Symbol library: `SNAP_Power_Mini.kicad_sym` (project `sym-lib-table`), the CN3163, written by the schematic generator.
- Footprint library: `SNAP_Power_Mini.pretty` (project `fp-lib-table`), written by `tools/make-footprints.py`: `WirePad_1x02_SMD_1.4x1.5mm_P2.0mm` (J1–J5), `SolderJumper-2_P0.9mm_Open_Pad0.6x0.8mm` (JP1) and `SolderJumper-2_P0.9mm_Bridged_Pad0.6x0.8mm` (JP2: the same pads joined by a 0.3 mm copper link, declared as a net tie so DRC accepts TEMP and GND touching). The jumpers' courtyards are only 0.1 mm outside their pads (never populated), which is what lets three vertical parts fit beside the IC.
- Images: `docs/images/` — `schematic.svg`, `pcb-top.png`, `pcb-bottom.png`, `pcb-iso.png`. `output/` (gitignored) holds ERC/DRC reports, the netlist and the fab package.

## Circuit

```
J4 SOLAR 1 ── VIN ─┬─ C1 10u ─ GND        U1 CN3163: VIN(4)  BAT(5)+FB(8) ── BAT+ ─┬─ C2 10u ─ GND
                   ├──────────────────────── VIN(4)                                 ├─ Q1 ─ J1 BATTERY 1
                   ├─ R4 4.7k ─ TEMP ─┬───── TEMP(1)                                └─ J2 SWITCH 1
                   │                  ├─ R5 47k ─ GND      ISET(2) ─ R1 1.2k ─ GND
                   │                  ├─ J5 NTC 1 (J5 2 ── GND)   GND(3), EP(9) ─ GND
                   │                  └─ JP2 (closed) ─ GND
                   └─ JP1 (open) ─ LED_PWR ─┬─ R2 1k ─ D1 red   ─ CHRG(7)
                                            └─ R3 1k ─ D2 green ─ DONE(6)
J2 SWITCH 2 ── VOUT ── J3 LOAD 1          J4 2, J1 2, J3 2 ── GND
J1 BATTERY 1 ── BAT_IN ── Q1 AO3401A drain(3) → source(2) ── BAT+;  gate(1) ── GND
```

Pinout 1 TEMP, 2 ISET, 3 GND, 4 VIN, 5 BAT, 6 ~DONE, 7 ~CHRG, 8 FB, exposed pad = GND. The PDF text extraction drops the overbarred names in the pin table; the 6/7 order comes from the description order and was cross-checked against wiki.adom.inc/ray/cn3163.

Design decisions:
- **Modelled on the SD05CRMA module** (CN3163, 4.4–6.5 V solar input, 1 A). No schematic of the module was found online. The circuit follows the datasheet's Figure 1, which such modules implement, including its two status LEDs: each open-drain output sinks an LED + resistor from VIN. The LEDs therefore light only while the panel supplies power; both are dark at night or with no panel.
- **0402 for the LEDs and their resistors:** 0603 parts did not fit beside the IC without growing the board.
- **Reverse-battery P-FET (Q1):** drain to the cell, source to BAT+, gate to GND. A correctly connected cell forward-biases the body diode, which pulls the source up to Vbat − 0.7 V, so Vgs ≈ −Vbat turns the channel on; from then on it conducts both ways (~60 mΩ at 4.5 V, ~85 mΩ at 2.5 V), so the charger still charges through it. A reversed cell sees an off FET with its body diode reverse-biased. No gate resistor or zener: |Vgs| ≤ 4.2 V against a 12 V rating. It sits on the cell side of everything, so the charger, switch and load are all protected.
- **LED enable jumper (JP1):** normally open, in the shared VIN feed to R2/R3, so the LEDs cost nothing unless bridged. One jumper for both LEDs (CHRG and DONE are never low together).
- **Battery NTC (R4, R5, J5, JP2):** the datasheet (pin table and "Battery Temperature Sense") suspends charging while TEMP is below 45 % or above 80 % of VIN (VLOW 42.5–47.5 %, VHIGH 77.5–82.5 %, input current ≤ 0.5 µA), and grounding TEMP disables the function. Its Figure 7 network is R1 from VIN to TEMP and R2 from TEMP to GND with the NTC in parallel; its formulas R1 = RTL·RTH·(k2 − k1) / ((RTL − RTH)·k1·k2) and R2 = RTL·RTH·(k2 − k1) / (RTL·(k1 − k1·k2) − RTH·(k2 − k1·k2)) with k1 = 0.45, k2 = 0.8 give 4.86k / 46.1k for a 10k B3950 NTC over 0–45 °C (RTL ≈ 33.6k, RTH ≈ 4.35k). With 4.7k / 47k the window is ~1–46 °C (a 10k B3435 NTC gives ~−2–49 °C). The window is ratiometric, so it doesn't depend on VIN. With no NTC fitted TEMP would sit at 91 % of VIN and block charging, hence JP2 closed by default; it costs ~1 mA through R4 while the panel is lit.
- **FB tied to BAT** gives the fixed 4.2 V float, with no Rx.
- **Charger on the cell side of the switch:** the CN3163 BAT pin feeds BAT+ directly, so the cell charges with the switch open and the switch only cuts the load. The alternative (charger after the switch) would power the load from the panel with the switch off.
- **Input limits:** CN3163 absolute max is 6.5 V on every pin; the operating input is 4.4–6 V. Use a 5 V panel whose open-circuit voltage stays below 6.5 V. "6 V" panels (Voc ≈ 7 V+) will damage it. No reverse-polarity or TVS protection on the panel input, same as the SD05CRMA; the datasheet suggests a TVS or Schottky for long panel cables.
- **Heat:** a linear charger dissipates (VIN − VBAT) × I, up to ~1.5–2 W at 1 A from a 5–6 V panel into a low cell. On a board this small the CN3163 will hit its ~130 °C thermal regulation and reduce the current by itself. That is expected behaviour, not a fault. Lower R1's current (raise R1) if that's a problem.
- **Symbol pin types:** CHRG/DONE are open_collector. VIN has a PWR_FLAG (it's fed from a passive pad); BAT+ has none (U1 BAT is power_out).
- J-pads are `Connector_Generic:Conn_01x02` with `in_bom no` (JP1's and JP2's symbols are `in_bom no` in the library); their footprint is `exclude_from_bom`/`exclude_from_pos_files`, and the two must agree or DRC parity complains.

## Board

14.4 × 12.2 mm, sharp corners (V-score panel friendly), no mounting holes, board top-left at (100, 100) in the editor. Copper is ≥0.5 mm from every edge; both GND zone outlines are inset 0.5 mm (an outline on the edge is itself a DRC error).

What sets the size: the top row of six pads needs 12.4 mm; the width is U1's courtyard (7.4 mm across the pins) plus three vertical 0402-size parts each side (3.4 mm). The height is two pad rows with their labels (3.2 mm each) around U1's courtyard (5.5 mm), plus 0.3 mm because Q1 in the bottom row is taller than the pads. The parts alone need ~90 mm² and the pads with labels ~70 mm² of the 176 mm², so there is little left to take out without dropping the labels or going double-sided.

- **Top row (y = 1.3):** J5 NTC at x = 2.2 (GND) / 4.2 (TEMP), J3 LOAD at 6.2 (−) / 8.2 (+), J2 SWITCH at 10.2 (VOUT) / 12.2 (BAT+). Pads 1.4 × 1.5 mm, 2 mm pitch. The TEMP pad sits straight above pin 1, the BAT+ switch pad near pin 8.
- **U1 at (7.2, 5.95) rot 0** (TEMP pin 1 top-left, VIN pin 4 bottom-left, BAT pin 5 bottom-right, FB pin 8 top-right).
- **Left of U1, vertical parts:** R4 (0.82, 4.17), R5 (1.86, 4.17), JP2 (2.88, 4.17) with their TEMP pads up; R1 (2.7, 7.0) below, ISET pad up.
- **Right of U1, vertical parts:** D1 (11.42, 4.17), R2 (12.46, 4.17), JP1 (13.48, 4.17); D2 (11.42, 6.11), R3 (12.46, 6.11); C2 horizontal at (12.42, 7.97) beside pin 5. D1's cathode faces down and D2's up, so both sit level with the tracks from pins 7 and 6.
- **Bottom row (y = H − 1.3 = 10.9):** J4 SOLAR at x = 1.25 (+) / 3.25 (−), C1 vertical at (4.98, 10.17) under pin 4, Q1 at (7.735, 10.395) rot 180 (drain left, source top-right under pin 5, gate bottom-right), J1 BATTERY at 11.15 (+) / 13.15 (−).

Routing (all hand-placed in `TRACKS` / `VIAS`; 0.4 mm power, 0.3 mm GND stubs, 0.25 mm signals, 0.6/0.3 mm vias):
- **F.Cu:** a 1.0 mm bridge J3.1→J2.2 (VOUT). VIN from the SOL+ pad along y = 9.5 (under the label) to C1 and up to pin 4; a 0.25 mm branch up the left edge (x = 0.82) to R4. TEMP straight down from the NTC pad into pin 1, with R4/R5/JP2 joined along their top pads. Q1's drain runs right, under Q1's own body between its source and gate pads, to the BAT+ pad. Q1's source goes up to a via and pin 5, then on to C2. The switch's BAT+ pad runs along y = 2.95 (under the labels) to a via and pin 8. Pins 7 and 6 go to the LED cathodes; R2.2, R3.2 and JP1's lower pad are joined (LED_PWR).
- **B.Cu jumpers:** BAT+ (via by pin 5 at (9.6, 8.7) straight up to the via by pin 8 at (9.6, 3.1)) and VIN (0.25 mm: via by C1 at (6.2, 9.25), along y = 9.65, up the right edge at x = 13.75 to a via above JP1 at (13.5, 2.85)). VIN crosses on the back because TEMP and BAT+ block the top band and Q1 blocks the bottom one.
- **Pours:** GND on both layers, solid pad connection. Six thermal vias in U1's exposed pad tie it to the B.Cu plane (datasheet layout note 4). The − pads carry their own vias (one in NTC−, two each in OUT−, SOL− and BAT−); C1 returns to SOL−, C2 to BAT−; R1, the R5/JP2 pair and Q1's gate each have a via.

Silkscreen: front — `NTC` / `-OUT+` / `SW` under the top row and `+SOL-` / `+BAT-` over the bottom row (0.8 mm). JP1 and JP2 have no labels (no room; the README points to them). Component references are hidden (no room). Back — "SNAP Mini Power" / "Controller v2.0" / "JaisorGadgets" (0.8 mm) in the bands between the vias; the full name no longer fits on one line at the 0.8 mm minimum. Re-check the back text against the vias after moving any.

## Regenerating the design

From the repo root:

```bash
"C:/Program Files/KiCad/10.0/bin/python.exe" tools/make-footprints.py   # only after changing a footprint
node tools/gen-schematic.js
kicad-cli sch erc --format json --severity-all -o output/erc.json SNAP_Power_Mini.kicad_sch
kicad-cli sch export netlist --format kicadxml -o output/net.xml SNAP_Power_Mini.kicad_sch
"C:/Program Files/KiCad/10.0/bin/python.exe" tools/build-pcb.py output/net.xml
kicad-cli pcb drc --schematic-parity --severity-all --format json -o output/drc.json SNAP_Power_Mini.kicad_pcb
```

`build-pcb.py` prints the pad centres of U1, R1–R5, C1/C2, D1/D2, JP1/JP2 and Q1; the track coordinates in `TRACKS` assume those positions, so re-check them if a placement changes.

Images:

```bash
kicad-cli sch export svg --exclude-drawing-sheet -o docs/images SNAP_Power_Mini.kicad_sch   # then rename SNAP_Power_Mini.svg -> schematic.svg
kicad-cli pcb render --side top --quality high -w 1600 -h 1000 -o docs/images/pcb-top.png SNAP_Power_Mini.kicad_pcb
kicad-cli pcb render --side bottom --quality high -w 1600 -h 1000 -o docs/images/pcb-bottom.png SNAP_Power_Mini.kicad_pcb
kicad-cli pcb render --side top --quality high --rotate "-40,0,25" --perspective -w 1600 -h 1000 -o docs/images/pcb-iso.png SNAP_Power_Mini.kicad_pcb
```

KiCad has no 3D model for the CN3163's footprint variant, so the renders show only its pads.

- Each script overwrites its output. **Once anyone edits the schematic or board in KiCad, stop regenerating that file**: port the edits into the scripts first, or switch to editing in KiCad.
- Before writing, check the editor for that file is closed (`~*.kicad_sch.lck` / `~*.kicad_pcb.lck`); afterwards the user must close the editor without saving and reopen.
- UUIDs in the schematic are deterministic (SHA-1 of the item key), so regenerating keeps the PCB footprint links stable.
